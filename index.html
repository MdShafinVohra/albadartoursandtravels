Frequently Asked Questions

What documents are required for Hajj/Umrah? +

You will need a valid passport (with at least 6 months validity), recent passport-sized photographs, proof of vaccination (meningitis and COVID-19), and a completed visa application form. We assist with all documentation.

How far in advance should I book? +

For Hajj, we recommend booking at least 6-8 months in advance due to limited quotas. For Umrah, 2-3 months in advance is usually sufficient, but earlier booking ensures better accommodation options.

Do you provide group discounts? +

Yes, we offer special discounts for groups of 10 or more people. Family packages and group rates are available for both Hajj and Umrah journeys.

What is included in your packages? +

Our packages typically include flights, visa processing, accommodation, transportation, meals (as per package), guided tours, and 24/7 support. Specific inclusions vary by package type.

Can you accommodate special dietary requirements? +

Yes, we can arrange meals according to specific dietary needs. Please inform us during the booking process about any special requirements.
